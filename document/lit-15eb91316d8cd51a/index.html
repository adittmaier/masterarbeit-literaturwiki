Begriffe und theoretische Grundlagen

  • Human knowledge takes many forms, including both descriptive knowledge of “what is” in the world, as well as prescriptive knowledge of “what might be”.
  • DSR aims to develop new knowledge that both provides utility in achieving human goals as well as having scientific legitimacy.
  • Classic work in the area appeared with Herbert Simon’s publication in 1996 of his Sciences of the Artificial, in which he called for a science of design that is intellectually tough and at least partly formalizable.
  • The term “design science” and focus on the artifact is thought to have been introduced in the 1960s by the inventor Buckminster Fuller, who called for a design science revolution based on technology, science and rationalism (see Cross, 2001).
  • The feature that distinguishes design science knowledge from other forms of knowledge is that it includes prescriptive statements that indicate how to do something to achieve a goal
